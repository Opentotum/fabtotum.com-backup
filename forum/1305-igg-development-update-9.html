    IGG Development update #9 May 26th 2014

    DEV. UPDATE#9
    Dear backers,
    Today we'll have a look at the production and supply chain status, but first let us proudly unveil the FABtotum Hybrid head.




    FABtotum Hybrid head V 1.0
    The Hybrid head is one of the last finalized parts of the Personal fabricator,
    We improved many specs of this part,starting from the onboard milling motor wich is now a 200W brushless beast, with custom-made high-precision ER-8 collet for improved quality.
    We started from something like this and we ended with this:



    Switching from subtractive to additive mode requires a fixed offset instead of manual adjustments. As you may also notice, enstops, lasers, lights and probe are fixed on the carriage so that any optional head can be cheaper and made with the FABtotum itself.





    Supply Chain Delays [Important!]
    As you know, one month ago we announced a delay of the production.
    We recevied a great support and understanding from you guys, and for that we are grateful. Leaving our backers with incomplete details could however generate more confusion than any honest "we're doing our best" statement.
    For this reason we are sharing with you most of our sensible internal plans.
    We hope, doing this, to shed some light on this complex process.





    Here you can see the current status of our suppliers: [1] [2]. We aren't terribly off or "tardi", considering the original shipping date was late May 2014. but it's a delay nonetheless.
    As you can see this is caused by all custom-made parts that require our contractors to machine parts for us, out of our control.


    A reasonable measurement for the first deliveries is perhaps 2 months from now,if everything goes right.
    Things are always moving fast: have a look at one of the most complex components being finished as we write: injection molds.



    Packaging & Welcome kit.
    We might be late but packaging and part of the welcome kit were ready for the release. the box is pretty sturdy and the welcome kit has been designed to get people into personal fabrication quickly.



    In other news
    We are preparing more documentation, images and videos to share with you.
    In the last Blog update we focused on the FABUI,expect more in the future.
    FABtotum also won the special jury prize at the FOCUS Innovation award this month. Thanks for voting!



    As always: your feedback is important: hit the comment section here or on our forums and speak your mind!

    See you next update and on the Blog!
    FABteam
