    Arduino Derivate: Vote to name!



    We asked our community to suggest a name for the FABtotum control board, our currently unnamed Arduino Mega Derivate.
    On Dec, 20th 2013 the submissions have been closed and now we are asking the community to help us decide what
    will be the name etched and/or printed on the PCB itself inside the FABtotum Personal Fabricator!
    Alongside with that the winner will receive a FABtotum T-shirt!

    The Rules


    1. List up to 5 names from the list below in a single post
    2. Can't vote yourself (duh!)
    3. Can't vote twice or change your preference!


    The most voted name will be the winner!

    Deadline:

    To vote, you have time until March 20th, 2014.
